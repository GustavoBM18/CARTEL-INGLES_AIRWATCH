Abstract

AirWatch is an integrated Internet of Things (IoT) environmental monitoring ecosystem paired with an e-commerce web platform and mobile application flow (Ink), created to detect water and air pollutants in agricultural irrigation canals and urban zones in Mixquiahuala de Juárez, Hidalgo.

The architecture combines ESP32 multi-sensor hardware nodes, cloud time-series telemetry, automated alerts, and regulatory compliance under Mexican IT standards to deliver accessible, real-time environmental intelligence.

Background & Problem
  • Canal Pollution: Irrigation channels in the Valle del Mezquital (such as El Bondho) receive untreated wastewater discharges containing heavy metals and organic contaminants.
  • Delayed Detection: Conventional water quality testing relies on slow manual sampling, limiting timely response for farmers and local authorities.
  • Lack of Accessible Kits: Modular, low-cost IoT monitoring hardware with integrated software support is scarce in regional agricultural communities.
Objective

To design, build, and validate the AirWatch IoT hardware prototype, web e-commerce platform (pag-integrador.pages.dev), and Ink user application flow for continuous water and air quality monitoring in Mixquiahuala de Juárez, ensuring information security and service quality compliance.

Regulatory Standards (Normativas)

Information security, telemetry integrity, and service governance in AirWatch are aligned with national Mexican standards:

NMX-I-27001-NYCE-2015 (SGSI)

Information Security Management System controls applied to protect user authentication, sensor API endpoints, cloud telemetry logs, and e-commerce transactions.

NMX-I-321-NYCE-2017 (IT Service Management)

Quality requirements for IT service delivery, incident response, sensor node availability, and continuous maintenance across subscription plans.

Environmental Water Quality Guidelines

Threshold calibration for pH, turbidity, TDS, and dissolved pollutants in agricultural irrigation channels based on CONAGUA benchmarks.

IoT Prototype Architecture

The AirWatch IoT Prototype consists of a waterproof field telemetry node deployed at critical canal checkpoints (El Bondho, Mixquiahuala) to capture and transmit real-time environmental metrics.

Microcontroller ESP32 Dual-Core Wi-Fi / BLE
Water Sensors pH, Turbidity, TDS & Temp
Air Quality Module MQ-135 / Gas & Particulate
Cloud & Protocols MQTT / HTTPS REST API

Conclusion

AirWatch successfully integrated an ESP32 IoT environmental monitoring prototype, an intuitive Ink user flow, and a functional e-commerce web portal aligned with NMX-I-27001-NYCE-2015 and NMX-I-321-NYCE-2017. Field validation in Mixquiahuala de Juárez confirms its capability to provide timely water and air pollution alerts with scalable commercial adoption.
